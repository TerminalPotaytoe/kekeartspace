Frequently Asked Questions

What art classes does KeKe's Art Space offer?

KeKe's Art Space offers creative art classes for children, professional drawing classes for teenagers, DSA art portfolio preparation and adult painting courses.

Our art studio is located at International Plaza, 10 Anson Road, #02-76, Singapore 079903.

Are beginners welcome?

Yes. Our art classes welcome beginners and experienced artists who want to improve their drawing and painting skills.

Do you provide DSA art portfolio preparation?

Yes. We provide guidance for students preparing their Direct School Admission (DSA) Art portfolio.
