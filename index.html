Frequently Asked Questions

What art classes does KeKe's Art Space offer?

We offer Creative Art classes for children, Professional & Advanced Drawing for teenagers, DSA Art Portfolio preparation, and Adult Art courses. Our lessons cover drawing, painting, sketching, watercolour, acrylic, and mixed media.

Where is KeKe's Art Space located?

Our studio is conveniently located at International Plaza, 10 Anson Road, #02-76, Singapore 079903, just a short walk from Tanjong Pagar MRT Station.

Are beginners welcome?

Absolutely. Our classes are suitable for complete beginners as well as students with prior experience. Lessons are tailored to each student's skill level and learning goals.

Do you provide DSA Art portfolio preparation?

Yes. We provide personalised coaching for students applying through the Direct School Admission (DSA) Art programme, including portfolio development, artwork refinement, and interview preparation.

What will I learn?

Students learn a wide range of artistic skills including sketching, drawing, colouring, watercolour, acrylic painting, composition, perspective, colour theory, and creative expression through guided projects.

How do I book a class?

Booking is simple. Open the Booking page, complete the form, and we'll contact you via WhatsApp to confirm your preferred class date and time.
