About Us

KeKe's Art Space is an art studio located at International Plaza, Singapore. We provide a welcoming environment where children, teenagers and adults can explore drawing, painting and creative expression.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced artist, our classes help you learn new techniques while enjoying the creative process.
